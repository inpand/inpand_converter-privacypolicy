Privacy Policy for INPAND CONVERT

Last updated: November 6, 2026

INPAND CONVERT ("we", "our", or "us"), operated by Inpand Works, is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our mobile application handles your documents, permissions, and data.

1. On-Device Local Processing

INPAND CONVERT prioritizes client-side on-device processing. Images, plain text files, CSVs, Markdown, and camera document scans are converted directly on your device using our local processing engine. For these formats, your files are not uploaded to our servers.

2. Optional Cloud Conversion for Office Formats

For complex Office formats (such as Microsoft Word DOCX and Excel XLSX), INPAND CONVERT provides an optional secure cloud conversion engine:

  • Transmission: Files are sent to our conversion server strictly over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3) connections.
  • Immediate Deletion: Files are stored only in a temporary directory while the conversion runs. That directory is automatically deleted immediately after the converted output is sent back to your device.
  • No Retention: We do not store, archive, log, inspect, or sell your documents. No user documents are retained on our servers.

3. Permissions Used

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used exclusively when you choose the "Scan to PDF" tool to photograph physical documents. Captured photos are processed locally on your phone and saved directly to your Downloads folder.
  • File Access (Storage Access Framework & System Photo Picker): INPAND CONVERT uses Android's native system file and photo pickers. The app does not request broad media storage permissions and only accesses files you explicitly pick.

4. No Third-Party Tracking or Ads

INPAND CONVERT does not track your location and does not display third-party advertisements. We do not collect your name, email, or account information. Our server may temporarily process technical data, such as IP address, as part of normal network operation.
